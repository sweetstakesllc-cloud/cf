How the report works

Profit before VAT is payments received minus refunds and recorded item costs, payment fees, shipping, packaging and business expenses. Profit after VAT subtracts recorded sales VAT from that same figure. VAT comes from Shopify or your order adjustment; the dashboard does not apply a fixed 25% deduction. The before-VAT figure still includes recorded sales VAT and is not the amount available to withdraw. Net sales exclude recorded VAT.

Refunds update the original order's date. Returned items reduce purchase costs only when Shopify records a restock. This is an order-based management report, not a payout statement.

Historical item costs are saved from Shopify on first import. Later product edits do not overwrite them. You can confirm or correct a cost inside an order. Missing costs and unreported fees remain flagged.

Shipping label expenses, chargebacks, third-party provider fees and Shopify billing charges may require manual entries. Empty fee data is never treated as confirmed zero.
