About us

Founded in Sweden in 2023, Circular Fash is committed to bringing high-quality, exclusive brands to the second-hand market. Our goal is to offer luxury at a reasonable price. To achieve that we continuously source and handpick premium pre-owned pieces from around the world, inspect their quality and refurbish them to a condition that closely resembles new.

There are three things we take extremely seriously.

  1. Authenticity. We always stand behind our products. Every item we receive goes through authentication before we even look at its quality, and we work with authentication companies such as Authentic Detective and Real Authentication. Read more about our process.
  2. Quality. This is what makes us different from other companies in our industry: we always inspect each item carefully to find any flaws or defects, looking at the front, the backside and the sleeves. The item is then cleaned following the recommendations on its washing label, so the material is never worn down in the process.
  3. Sustainability. Sustainability is at the core of our business. By extending the life cycle of clothing, shoes and accessories, we help reduce waste and cut down on CO2 emissions. With every purchase, our customers take part in a smarter, more sustainable way to shop fashion.

So who are we serving? Everyone who loves premium second-hand fashion — people who would rather have several pieces for the price of one at full retail price, without giving up the feeling that it is like new. No one would notice it is pre-owned.
