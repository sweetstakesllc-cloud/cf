About Us

A small studio in Sweden giving exceptional pieces a second life — and a daily live stream that makes buying pre-owned feel like shopping with a friend who knows their stuff.

Why We Started

Luxury fashion is built to last decades. Most of it doesn't get the chance — it sits in a wardrobe, unworn, because selling it privately is tedious and buying pre-owned online means trusting a stranger's word.

Circular Fash exists to remove both problems. We buy and consign pieces we would carry ourselves, verify every one of them, and sell them at a fraction of retail to people who will actually use them.

A bag that gets carried for another ten years is a better outcome than a bag that gets manufactured.

How We Work

  • Everything is inspected in person. Nothing is listed from photographs. Read about our process.
  • We grade honestly. Wear is photographed, not hidden. The condition on the listing is the condition in the box.
  • We price against retail. Every listing shows the estimated retail price so you can see exactly what you're saving.
  • We show up live, every day. At 19:00 we go on TikTok and show pieces up close — the detail photos can't do everything.

The Live

The daily stream started as a way to answer questions about condition. It turned into the heart of the business. You see the piece in someone's hands, at real scale, with the marks and the patina visible — and you can ask about anything before you commit.

It runs every day at 19:00 CET. See this week's schedule.

Circular by Design

Buying pre-owned keeps a piece in circulation and out of a landfill, and it doesn't cost anything to make. That's the whole argument for second-hand luxury, and it happens to also be the cheaper way to own something good.

We ship from Sweden to the world, with 14-day returns on everything.
