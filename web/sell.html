Selling Questions

What do you accept?

Authenticated luxury and premium designer pieces — bags, outerwear, knitwear, accessories and small leather goods. Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Moncler, Burberry, Dior, Celine, Fendi, Prada, Canada Goose, Stone Island and similar.

We don't take fast fashion, heavily damaged items, or anything we can't verify.

What do I earn?

Your share depends on the final selling price — higher-value pieces earn a higher percentage. We agree the split and the listing price with you in writing before the item goes live, so there are no surprises.

How do I send my items?

Once we've reviewed your submission we send a prepaid shipping label for Swedish addresses. Pack the item securely with any original dust bag, box or paperwork — those raise the price it sells for.

How long does it take to sell?

Most pieces sell within a few weeks. Items shown on the daily TikTok live tend to move fastest — we feature new consignments on stream regularly.

What if you can't authenticate it?

If we can't verify an item, we won't list it. We return it to you at our cost and explain what we found.
